# Access and licensing

No project license has been selected or granted. This locally prepared package is for author review and validation. It must not be represented as an approved public release.

Original source code, numerical outputs, learned policies, benchmark specifications and external biological inputs are distinct rights categories. A hash verifies identity, not copyright ownership or permission. Existing scientific publications or accessible inputs do not by themselves establish permission to redistribute all derived material.

The package contains project implementations, computational explanations, synthetic records, reported scalar numerical results and selected learned policy arrays. These are retained privately pending the authors' licensing and derived-output decisions. It excludes melanoma input CSV/protocol/manifest files and full state-bearing biological trajectories/restart states. Their fingerprints and permitted-local-use checks are included instead.

The metadata in `manifests/rights_scope.json` gives the category decisions and explicit holds. The complete per-file export and permission assessment is retained separately with the authors, not mixed into the computational package. No third-party dependency, wheel, font or TeX distribution is vendored.

Before public release, the authors must settle source-code licensing, derived-output and model/input redistribution, required attributions and release authorization. Where exact inputs cannot lawfully be supplied, provide an authorized acquisition route and retain the resulting coverage limitation. Do not silently substitute a different input.
